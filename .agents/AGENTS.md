# ManakAI UI / Product Design Direction

These rules are strictly binding for all frontend UI/UX development across the ManakAI project.

## 1. Desired Visual Character
- **Theme**: Government / Public-Service Portal aesthetic.
- **Tone**: Professional, institutional, trustworthy, formal but modern.
- **Palette**: Restrained. Use white/light neutral backgrounds with navy/dark blue institutional accents.
- **Styling**: Subtle borders, clear typography hierarchy, restrained cards, structured navigation, minimal visual noise, and minimal animations.
- **Components**: Emphasize information-first design with tables, checklists, clear status indicators, and professional forms where appropriate.

## 2. Strictly Avoid
- **DO NOT** make ManakAI look like a generic AI chatbot (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity), AI SaaS, futuristic dashboard, or startup landing page.
- **AVOID**: Neon gradients, purple/pink "AI" gradients, glowing effects, excessive glassmorphism, floating cards, overly rounded containers, animated backgrounds, robot illustrations, or AI brain graphics.
- **DO NOT** use conversational-chat interfaces as the primary visual identity. The AI should feel functional, not flashy.

## 3. Branding Rule
- **Independence**: ManakAI is independent. Do NOT imitate BIS branding closely enough to imply official ownership.
- **Prohibitions**: No BIS logo, no official BIS header, no official BIS seal, no fake government emblems, no fake department identity.
- **Disclaimer**: Always include the exact disclaimer: *"ManakAI is an independent technology prototype providing AI-assisted guidance based on publicly available BIS information. For official decisions and services, please refer to BIS."*

## 4. Content Style
- **Prefer**: Formal, service-oriented labels ("Search Standards", "Compliance Guidance", "Required Documents", "Next Steps").
- **Avoid**: Marketing language or "AI magic" phrases ("Ask our AI anything", "Supercharge compliance", "Instant AI answers").

## 5. Responsive Design & Feature Consistency
- Maintain the public-service aesthetic consistently across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.
- Avoid overly dense dashboard layouts; prioritize readability and evidence visibility.
- **Feature Consistency**: Every new feature (Scan & Verify, Know Before You Buy, Complaint Copilot) MUST use the exact same institutional design system. Do not create separate visual products for individual features.
- **Primary Design Principle**: The user should feel, *"I am using a reliable compliance/public-service information platform that happens to use AI internally,"* NOT *"I am chatting with an AI app."*
